Welcome

A Royal Welcome
In Paris

Set in Ivry-sur-Seine, 7 Km du centre de Paris, Hôtel Royal pairs classical detailing with a calmer, more discreet hospitality style. Gilded accents, chandeliers, and quiet lounges create an atmosphere that feels distinctive without becoming theatrical.

With nearby metro, RER, and bus connections, the hotel offers a more practical Paris stay: close enough to move through the city easily, yet removed enough to return each evening to a quieter rhythm.

Whether you are here for a romantic escape, a family adventure, or a business stay, we are honoured to be your home in Paris.
